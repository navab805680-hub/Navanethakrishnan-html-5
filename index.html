Journey Through Space

Exploring the Universe

Space exploration allows scientists to learn more about planets, stars, galaxies, and other objects beyond Earth. Modern technology has made it possible to study distant regions of the universe.

The Moon was the first major destination for human space exploration. Astronauts have traveled to the lunar surface and conducted experiments to understand its environment.

Mars is another important target for exploration. Scientists study the planet to discover whether it once had conditions suitable for life and to prepare for possible future missions.

Satellites play an important role in modern life. They are used for communication, weather forecasting, navigation, television broadcasting, and observing changes on Earth.

Space telescopes help astronomers observe objects that are extremely far away. They provide valuable information about the formation of stars, planets, and galaxies.
